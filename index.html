Older phone and it won't install? Get the 32-bit version.

  1. Tap the button on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app, then tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway. It does this for any app outside the Play Store.

To update, download again and install over the old version. Your data stays.
